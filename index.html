About Me

I am a research scholar at IIT Kharagpur, working on the design and engineering of functional materials for catalytic and energy-related applications. My research combines experimental studies, advanced characterization and computational approaches to understand structure–property relationships in materials.


By training, I am a chemist, with Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Chemistry, followed by an M. Tech in Materials Science and Engineering. This interdisciplinary background allows me to bridge fundamental chemistry with materials engineering.
