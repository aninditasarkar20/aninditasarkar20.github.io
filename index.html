About Me

I am a research scholar at IIT Kharagpur, working in the field of engineering functional materials in energy conversion.

My research interests include materials engineering for energy conversion through atomic-scale investigations, electrocatalytic activity and density functional theory with the goal of establishing structure-property correlations.
