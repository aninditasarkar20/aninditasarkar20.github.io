About Me

I am a research scholar at IIT Kharagpur, working on the design and engineering of functional materials for catalytic applications. My research explores the relationship between materials structure and properties through the combination of experimental studies, advanced characterization, and computational approaches.

By training, I am a chemist, with Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Chemistry, followed by an M. Tech in Materials Science and Engineering. This interdisciplinary background allows me to bridge fundamental chemistry with the engineering of advanced functional materials with catalytic activity.

Research Interests

Synthesis of functional Materials

Design and engineering of functional materials for energy-related applications.

Electron Microscopy

Atomic-scale investigation of the materials.

Electrocatalysis

Investigation of electrocatalytic activity of materials for energy conversion.

Density Functional Theory

First principle investigation of the structural, electronic, and catalytic properties of funtional materials.
